## 3. Refusals, warnings, notes and caveats

### Refusals

#### `complete_confounding` (refuse, stage S6)

- **Explanation:** Every unit given one treatment level also shares a recorded nuisance level, such as a chamber, with no unit of the other treatment level; for example, all runs of one recipe were in one chamber and all runs of the other recipe in another. A treatment difference and a chamber difference would then produce exactly the same data, so no analysis can separate them. (For a numbered factor such as run order, the same happens when the treatment lines up exactly with a straight-line trend in it.) Measuring more wafers or sites does not help, because it adds no comparison of the treatments within a chamber. The design has to change so that every treatment level runs at every level of that factor, as in the reassignment suggestion when one is given.
- **Technical detail:** Comparison 'high_vs_low' is not estimable once 'chamber' is included: high - low cannot be separated from 'chamber' differences (design-matrix rank; table [[4, 0], [0, 4]]). The data contain no temp comparison made within a level of 'chamber'. More measurements below the 'run' level cannot restore that missing independent information. A crossed, randomized assignment of temp levels to chamber levels is needed.
