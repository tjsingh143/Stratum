## 3. Refusals, warnings, notes and caveats

### Warnings

#### `sd_ratio` (warn, stage S8)

- **Explanation:** The largest spread of unit averages within a treatment level is at least twice the smallest. The analysis assumes a similar spread at every level, so read its results with some caution. The factor of two is a practical rule of thumb, and with few units per level the spread itself is poorly known, so this is a prompt to look, not a verdict.
- **Technical detail:** wafer means: largest-to-smallest SD of unit means = 5.543 (warning at >= 2.0, a pragmatic v1 threshold). Warning only.

### Report caveats (they do not change the evidence decision)

#### `assignment_not_randomized` (caveat)

- **Explanation:** The declaration says this factor's levels were not assigned at random: they were assigned systematically, the method was not stated, or the factor is an inherent property such as a position on the wafer. The test and intervals are computed the same way regardless. What changes is the interpretation: without randomization, a difference may partly reflect something else that varied along with the factor, so it should not automatically be read as caused by it.
- **Technical detail:** Factor 'etch_time': assignment.method is 'not_stated' in the declaration (The package documentation says etch time has 4 levels and wafers are nested in etch time; it does not state how etch times were assigned to wafers.). The tests and intervals are computed the same way for every assignment method; the caveat concerns attributing a difference to the factor (D-039).
