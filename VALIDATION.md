# Stratum validation

**Portable-verification follow-up:** scenario replay now distinguishes finite-float
agreement from byte identity; see [the policy](REPRODUCIBILITY.md#portable-scenario-verification).
Ten new regression tests protect exact decisions, provenance, artifact structure and
report/write-up content. This update changes no calculation, frozen assertion, stored
scientific result or simulation evidence. The measurements below retain their recorded
environment and dates; strict-byte mode still exposes the documented S3 difference.

**The software checks pass while statistical limitations remain.** This document reports
two kinds of evidence and keeps them separate:

- **Checks run on this repository.** Every command in the last section was run on this
  snapshot before publication and passed.
- **Evidence retained from the original v1.0.0 release.** The frozen simulation study, its
  per-attempt ledgers, the archive of its first execution and the release gate that
  reconciled them depend on material that is not part of this repository. Their results
  are reported here, with selected machine-readable extracts in
  [`validation/evidence/`](validation/evidence/), but they were not re-run for this snapshot.

The frozen synthetic study contains 209 cells × 2,000 seeded attempts = 418,000 distinct
attempts. These are Monte Carlo attempts, not 418,000 software tests.

## Frozen simulation design and scoring

The grid spans T1/T2/T3, upper replication of 2, 3 and 5, lower counts including 1, 2 and
4 wafers and 1, 5 and 9 sites where applicable, null and non-null effects, balanced blocks
and adverse conditions. Unsupported collapsed T1 structures remain refusal cases. Adverse
constructions include missing sites, no replication, complete and partial confounding,
aliased grouping, unequal upper variances, heavy tails, outliers and run-order drift. These
parametric synthetic constructions establish behavior under specified inputs, not process
realism. The synthetic data generator is included as
[`validation/wp6_generate.py`](validation/wp6_generate.py).

Seeds, definitions, expected outcomes and criteria were fixed before simulation. Formal
alerts use exact simultaneous binomial cell bands within each nominal family. Two-Monte-Carlo-SE
cell and pooled flags are separate diagnostics; separate families do not establish a
project-wide simultaneous guarantee. Adverse cells are descriptive and do not acquire a
post-result acceptance criterion.

Primary coverage denominators retain unavailable intervals as noncoverage. Unavailable
tests contribute no rejection. Expected structural refusals have no coverage or
false-positive metric. Null-test false-positive rates and non-null rejection rates are
distinct. At 2,000 attempts the nominal MCSE for a 5% rate is 0.4873 percentage points. The
evidence includes both nominal and empirical MCSE.

## Formal alerts and the interval investigation

T1 run coverage spans **1,164/2,000 (58.20%) to 1,833/2,000 (91.65%)**. Its pooled
75.1625% coverage includes 4,755 unavailable intervals. Wafer intervals err in both
directions despite their pooled 95.3375% rate. The
[12 conforming T1 cell records](validation/evidence/t1_component_cells.json) preserve
availability, negative estimates, bias, boundary counts and metrics. Their frozen
acceptable coverage band is 1,871–1,927 successes per 2,000 attempts. Every alert, with its
band and observed count, is listed in
[formal_alerts_and_corrections.json](validation/evidence/formal_alerts_and_corrections.json).

The [24 deterministic component calculations](validation/evidence/interval_investigation.json)
investigate this failure using independent chi-square strata. Nonpositive moment estimates
already prevent nominal coverage in several small designs; the positive-estimate
approximation adds error. The original release gate re-ran this calculation; its script and
inputs are not part of this repository. It adds no Monte Carlo attempts and does not repair
the interval method. **Approximate upper-component intervals cannot be treated as reliably
calibrated 95% intervals over this grid.** Generated reports carry this caveat beside every
component interval.

All 74,000 expected refusals occurred, with zero unexpected refusals. The exact two-sided
95% binomial interval is [99.99501515201239%, 100%]; a zero empirical MCSE at 100% is not
certainty. There were zero exceptions and zero expectation mismatches in the corrected
study, 229,041 WARN attempts, and 3,153 component intervals with endpoint overflow.
Component events can overlap within a fit and must not be added as distinct attempts.
Overflow intervals remain mathematically available and are scored in log space.

The first execution of the study had **11,933 serialization failures** when recording
unequal-site-count results. The audit serialization was repaired and the study re-run on
identical inputs: the corrected record has the same 418,000 input fingerprints and 406,067
unchanged records for the unaffected attempts
([history reconciliation](validation/evidence/validation_history.json)). Zero failed fits in
the corrected study do not erase the earlier execution failure. Before random execution, a
within-plot cancellation check that refused three deterministic T3 batch-label layouts,
contrary to their frozen WARN expectation, was also repaired; both corrections are listed in
[formal_alerts_and_corrections.json](validation/evidence/formal_alerts_and_corrections.json).
No degenerate attempts occurred in the simulation; zero-error paths have deterministic
tests, not an empirical calibration claim from this grid.

## Published-reference agreement and remaining differences

The [WP3 reproduction](validation/WP3_REPRODUCTION.md) ([JSON](validation/wp3_reproduction.json))
retains **198/199** frozen pass/fail matches, **40/40** supplementary matches and one failed
comparison. The failure (D-070 in the outputs) is a Semiconductor restricted-likelihood
reporting difference: Stratum 50.65049503571727 versus the stored criterion
72.8312048136367; the difference 22.180709777919432 exceeds the tolerance
0.007283120481363671. The difference equals `32 log 2`. Full-covariance calculations support
the stated convention; it affects neither fitted components nor tests. The comparison
remains failed.

The source verifier also retains the NIST differences F26, F27 and L13 rather than
replacing printed values. Three unobtained book references (O19, O20 and S06) remain
unavailable; package transcripts are not presented as independently checked book pages.
Semiconductor's SD-ratio warning and lithography's studentized-residual warning remain
attached to the published analyses. Source and licensing caveats are in
[reference/README.md](reference/README.md).
