## 3. Refusals, warnings, notes and caveats

### Report caveats (they do not change the evidence decision)

#### `assignment_not_randomized` (caveat)

- **Explanation:** The declaration says this factor's levels were not assigned at random: they were assigned systematically, the method was not stated, or the factor is an inherent property such as a position on the wafer. The test and intervals are computed the same way regardless. What changes is the interpretation: without randomization, a difference may partly reflect something else that varied along with the factor, so it should not automatically be read as caused by it.
- **Technical detail:** Factor 'position': assignment.method is 'inherent' in the declaration (Position is a property of the measurement location.). The tests and intervals are computed the same way for every assignment method; the caveat concerns attributing a difference to the factor (D-039).
